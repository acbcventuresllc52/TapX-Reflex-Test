Privacy Policy

TapX: Reflex Test

No personal data collected

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TapX: Reflex Test (package name com.chrimsonedgeassociates.tapx), published by ACBC Ventures LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

In short: TapX does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app works entirely on your device.

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. TapX does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not request your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal data.

Data Stored on Your Device

To make the game work, TapX saves a small amount of non-personal data locally on your device only:

  • Your best score for each game mode
  • The game mode you selected last

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

TapX does not request any Android permissions. The app does not need internet access, and it does not connect to any server.

Third-Party Services

TapX does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or social media services. No third-party code receives information about you through the app.

Personal data collectedNone

Children's Privacy

Because TapX does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no purchases.

Google Play

If you download TapX from Google Play, Google may process certain information about your device and account in accordance with its own privacy policy. We do not receive that information through the app. You can read Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Security

Since all game data stays on your device and is never transmitted, the app does not expose your information to external systems. The security of the stored data depends on the security of your device.
